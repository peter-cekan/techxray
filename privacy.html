Privacy Policy

Tech X-Ray Analytics — last updated October 11, 2026

Who uses this application

Tech X-Ray Analytics is a personal tool used only by the owner of the Tech X-Ray YouTube channel. It is not available to other users.

Data accessed

With the owner's authorization, the application reads, in read-only mode, the statistics of the owner's own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API and the YouTube Analytics API: video list, titles, publication dates, views, watch time, audience retention and similar aggregated metrics. It never modifies, uploads or deletes any content.

How data is used and stored

The data is used only to analyze the channel's performance and plan future videos. It is stored on the owner's own computer. It is not sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Use of YouTube data is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Contact

Questions can be sent through the Tech X-Ray YouTube channel.
